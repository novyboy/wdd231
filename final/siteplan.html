Siteplan

Site Name

Nova's Art—this name uses alliteration to make a snappy title for a website that one can use to see Nova's art.

Site Purpose

This site provides public access to completed and w.i.p. art by Nova.

Scenarios

  • Can I get a physical copy of the art?
  • Can I make use your art?
